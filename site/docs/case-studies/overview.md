# Documentation Engineering Case Studies

These case studies show how I identify documentation needs, organize complex information, and build content that people can use and maintain. They include independent public projects and summaries of work in larger product environments.

## About these examples

My long-term documentation work belongs to the companies I worked for, with restrictions on access and publication. The enterprise case studies describe my work and outcomes without publishing the internal source material.

I created the public projects because I saw needs I could address. They also give me a way to show my reasoning, the alternatives I considered, and the work itself. Euler is an independent proposal with a working demo. TeraCreators Help is a published community guide, with community feedback and later revisions.
